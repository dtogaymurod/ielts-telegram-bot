CUE CARD: CHILDHOOD FRIEND

May-Dec 2026 Latest Cue Card Pool
PART 2: INDIVIDUAL LONG TURN (CUE CARD)
1. Official Cue Card Prompt
📌 Describe a friend from your childhood
You should say:
• who this person is
• how and when you met each other
• what activities you enjoyed doing together
• and explain why you liked him or her so much.
2. 1-Minute Brainstorming & Note-Taking Strategy
• Who / What: Bitta aniq shaxs yoki obyektni tanlang (ortiqcha o'ylanib vaqt yo'qotmang).
• Past Habitual ('used to' / 'would'): Tarixi va dastlabki xotiralar.
• Specific Anecdote (Misol): 1 ta esda qolarli voqeani batafsil hikoya qilish.
• Reflection / Emotions: Oxirida shaxsiy xulosa va tuyg'ular.
3. Band 7.5 - 9.0 Lexical Resource (Storytelling Vocabulary)
# Word / Idiom Meaning (Uzbek) Context Usage in Story
1 Kindred spirit (idiom) Qalb yaqini, hamfikr inson From our very first conversation, I knew he was a true kindred spirit.
2 Through thick and thin Barcha qiyinchilik va shodliklarda He is the kind of loyal friend who stands by you through thick and thin.
3 Inseparable bond Ajralmas mustahkam do'stlik Growing up in the same neighborhood, we formed an almost inseparable bond.
4 Break the ice Tanishuvdagi noqulaylikni yo'qotish We broke the ice on the first day of primary school by sharing sketchbooks.
5 Drift apart (phrasal verb) Asta-sekin uzoqlashmoq Although distance caused us to drift apart slightly, our connection remains intact.

4. Full 2-Minute Model Monologue (Band 8.5+)
🎯 Complete Sample Speech (approx. 220 words / 2 minutes):
I would like to talk about a close childhood companion of mine named Anvar, with whom I shared my most memorable formative years. We first crossed paths back in primary school when we were roughly seven years old. I vividly recall feeling quite apprehensive on the first day of grade one, and by sheer coincidence, we were seated next to each other. We quickly broke the ice by sharing our sketchbooks, and from that day forward, an inseparable friendship blossomed.

Growing up in the same neighborhood, we spent virtually every single afternoon together. After completing our homework, we would cycle down to the local park, organize impromptu football matches, and build imaginative treehouses. What made our camaraderie truly distinctive was our mutual fascination with astronomy and science fiction books; we would spend hours stargazing and discussing space exploration.

The primary reason I have always held him in such high esteem is his unwavering integrity and altruistic nature. He was never the kind of fair-weather friend who vanishes during hardships; rather, he stood by me through thick and thin. Even when we occasionally had petty squabbles, we would resolve them swiftly without harboring any resentment. Although our university pursuits have placed us in different cities today, whenever we reconnect, it feels as though no time has passed at all.
5. Examiner Follow-up Questions (Rounding-off)
Follow-up 1: Do you still keep in contact with this friend today?
Band 8.5+ Quick Response: "Yes, absolutely. Despite our hectic schedules in different cities, we catch up over video calls every couple of weeks and always make time to meet during national holidays."
Follow-up 2: Do you think childhood friendships are different from adult friendships?
Band 8.5+ Quick Response: "Unquestionably. Childhood bonds are formed organically through play without any professional pretenses, making them far purer and more resilient."
